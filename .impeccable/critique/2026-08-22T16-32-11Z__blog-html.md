## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM.** Generic dark-mode reading surface — strip RG's logo, footer, and gold accent and the hero "Ideas, marcas y estrategia" could sit on any marketing blog. No Puebla/México signal, no editorial stance, no link back to RG's own 5-service taxonomy. The brand tokens don't even resolve: `blog.css` reads `--accent / --text-primary / --text-muted`, none of which exist in `index.css` (which defines `--accent-color / --text-color / --bg-color`). Every blog color hard-codes its fallback, producing a private gold palette that drifts from the rest of the site's orange. The post view itself is the one authored block (760px column, 1.75 line-height, italic excerpt, gold blockquote, code styling), but the bottom of the post is dead.

**Deterministic scan.** Ran in DEGRADED regex mode (HTML parser modules `htmlparser2`, `css-select`, `css-tree`, `domutils` unavailable) → lower bound. 7 raw hits: 6 × `overused-font` (Montserrat/Poppins — false positives, intentional brand stack per AGENTS.md) and 1 × `side-tab` on the blockquote `border-left: 3px solid var(--accent, #c89b3c)` (false positive — site-wide blockquote convention). The rule families that would matter here (computed contrast, selector-aware checks, custom-property resolution) did not run.

**Browser overlays.** Assessment B installed Playwright + Chromium on the fly and captured screenshots against `python -m http.server 8765` at 1366×900 for both views. Zero console errors, zero failed requests. Browser-only findings: (a) draft copy `Prueba 1, subiendo con...` published on the list; (b) featured post `simulador-de-servicios` has a body about brand distintivos (mismatched slug/body); (c) that post's cover is a Game of Thrones still; (d) `#blogTagFilter` only populates 3 of 5 service categories; (e) inline `onclick` on the back button; (f) card byline hard-codes `RG Gestión de Marcas` and post byline hard-codes `Por Gustavo Martinez.` with a trailing period.

## Overall Impression

The surface is *engineered*, not *authored*. The Markdown→sanitize→render pipeline is wired correctly, the card grid is responsive, the dark-mode typography is restrained. What the page does not have is any fingerprint of RG Gestión de Marcas: no Puebla voice, no service-threaded IA, no editorial bottom-of-post, no real featured curation, and the content layer is still in pre-launch state.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Content layer is leaking pre-launch state.** Draft copy published; featured post slug/title don't match its body; cover is a GOT still. Audit `posts` in Supabase; unpublish/draft anything with placeholder content, fix the slug/body pairing on the simulator post, replace the GOT cover, add a quality gate before flipping status to `published`.
2. **[P0] Cards unreachable by keyboard/AT.** `.blog-card` (`js/blog.js:181-182`) attaches `click` to an `<article>` with no role, no tabindex, no keydown handler, no inner anchor. Keyboard users cannot open regular posts. Fix: turn cover + title into `<a href="./blog.html?slug=…">` and drop the parent click handler.
3. **[P0] Slug-not-found renders a half-broken page.** `js/blog.js:243-249` unhides the list view without calling `renderList()`, then writes a red error sentence into `#blogState`. Grid + featured are empty. Fix: call `renderList()` first, then overwrite the state message, or render a dedicated not-found block with "Volver al blog" + 3 recent posts.
4. **[P1] Brand token mismatch — two accents on the same site.** `blog.css` reads `--accent / --text-primary / --text-muted`; `index.css` defines `--accent-color / --text-color / --bg-color`. None of the blog tokens resolve; page hard-codes `#c89b3c` while the rest of the site uses `#f4a71c`. Fix: rename blog variables to match the globals, or add the three missing tokens to `:root` in `index.css`.
5. **[P1] Dead post bottom.** After the tag chips at `js/blog.js:280-286`, no author, no read time, no share, no related posts, no CTA tying back to services. Fix: add `Publicado por [author] · [min] min de lectura`, two related-post cards (re-query Supabase for posts sharing any tag), and a soft CTA card to `contacto.html` or `simulador-servicios.html`.
6. **[P2] "Destacado" is a lie.** The featured card is always `postsToRender[0]` (`js/blog.js:134`) — labelled "Destacado · 15 ene 2026." Fix: relabel as "Más reciente" or add a real `featured: boolean` column on `posts`.
7. **[P2] Tag filter only shows 3 of 5 service categories.** `#blogTagFilter` populates from the union of tags actually attached to published posts. Fix: pre-seed with the canonical 5 services from AGENTS.md, then add any extra tags the staff creates on top.

## Minor Observations

- `blog.html:103` back button uses inline `onclick`; `<button>` should not need inline JS, and `history.back()` would preserve list state.
- Featured-card is a click target *and* contains a `<a class="blog-featured__cta">` — nested interactive, fires twice on activation.
- `aria-live="polite"` on `#blogFeatured` + `#blogGrid` is too aggressive.
- Tag chips on cards (`blog.js:204`) and on the post (`blog.js:283`) are inert `<span>`s.
- `cover_image` injected as inline `style="background-image:url(...)"` with only `"` escaped — `)` not escaped, scheme not validated. Safe today, fragile.
- `el()` helper's `html:` branch (`blog.js:41`) is dead code on the public surface.
- `particles-js` runs under the reading surface without honoring `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Card byline hard-codes `RG Gestión de Marcas` (`blog.js:210`); post byline hard-codes `Por Gustavo Martinez.`. No structured `author` field.
- No OG image / Twitter card image; no JSON-LD `BlogPosting` schema.
- No TOC / heading anchors for long posts.
- Floating-social toggle has `aria-label` but does not toggle `aria-expanded`.

## Questions to Consider

1. If a stranger landed on `blog.html` and you deleted the RG logo, footer, and gold accent — would they have any way to know this is a Puebla marketing agency's blog? What's the one sentence RG wants every blog visitor to take away?
2. Two brand accents live in the same site today (gold in `blog.css`, orange in `index.css`). Which is the *real* RG accent — and does anyone on the team know?
3. The post view's bottom is dead. That is the moment a reader is most willing to convert. Is "ending the post with anything" (author + read time + 2 related + CTA) a better ROI than redesigning the hero?
